## What is WhatsApp automation for business?

WhatsApp automation for business is a layer on top of your WhatsApp Business number that reads incoming messages and responds for you, in your brand's voice, without a person tapping out every reply. Think of it as a front desk that lives inside the app your customers already use every day.

At its simplest it sends an instant acknowledgement so nobody sits staring at a single grey tick. At its most useful it holds a real conversation: it answers your five most common questions, works out whether the person is a genuine enquiry or a supplier or spam, collects the details you need, and offers a booking slot. When the chat needs a human, it stops and passes a clean, summarised thread to your team.

The important distinction is that this runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform (the WhatsApp Business API), not on a phone propped up in the back office running an unofficial tool. Official means your number will not get banned for automated sending, your templates are approved by Meta, and the whole thing scales past the one conversation a human can hold at a time.

## What can you actually automate on business WhatsApp?

You can automate the whole first mile of a conversation, from the instant reply to a confirmed appointment, and leave your team only the chats that genuinely need a person. Here is what that covers in practice for a local business.

- **Instant first response.** Every message gets an answer in seconds, at 2pm or 2am, so no enquiry cools off waiting for Monday.
- **Frequently asked questions.** Opening hours, prices, location, parking, whether you take a certain treatment or service. The questions you answer twenty times a day.
- **Lead qualification.** A few short questions that tell you what the person actually wants and whether they are ready to book, before your team spends a minute on them.
- **Appointment booking.** The automation checks a live calendar, offers real slots, and writes the confirmed booking back so it appears on your team's diary and in your CRM.
- **Reminders and follow-ups.** Approved template messages that confirm a booking, remind the day before, and nudge a no-reply back into the conversation.
- **Click-to-chat entry points.** A link or QR code on your website, Google profile, ads, and receipts that opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled, so starting a chat takes one tap.

That last one is worth setting up even before the full automation. ## How do you set up WhatsApp automation for your business?

You set it up in five steps, and with a managed provider most of the heavy lifting is done for you. The realistic timeline for a local business is about a week from kickoff to live, not the multi-month project people often fear.

Here is the checklist, in order:

1. **Get a WhatsApp Business Platform (API) account.** This is the official, automation-ready version, separate from the free WhatsApp Business app on your phone. A managed setup handles the Meta Business verification and connects a phone number for you.
2. **Map your top conversations.** Write down the ten questions you answer most and the path from "hello" to "booked". This is the single biggest input, and it is the part only you can supply.
3. **Build the flows and message templates.** Your provider turns that map into conversation flows and submits the notification templates (confirmations, reminders) to Meta for approval.
4. **Connect your calendar and CRM.** So a booking made in chat becomes a real appointment on the diary and a clean record in your system, not another thing to copy across by hand.
5. **Test, set the human handover, and go live.** Run real scenarios, decide exactly when the bot should step back and pass a chat to a person, then switch it on and watch the first conversations.

The one step you cannot outsource is step two. The automation is only as good as the answers and rules you feed it, so spend an afternoon getting your FAQs and booking logic clear. Everything after that is configuration a provider like Automis does with you.
