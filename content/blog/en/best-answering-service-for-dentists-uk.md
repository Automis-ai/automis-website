## How did I pick these?

I looked for services a UK practice can buy today and judged them on what a dental front desk loses sleep over. This is my judgement, and I stuck to what each vendor says on its own pages.

- Whether the call ends in a booking or only in a message. A message still needs someone to ring back, and the patient may already have rung the next practice.
- Cover outside the hours your team is in. Plenty of new-patient calls land at 7pm or on a Sunday.
- A price you can find and read. Where a vendor hides it, I say "price on request" and tell you what to ask.
- A route to a human for emergencies, upset patients and clinical questions.

I left out services I could not read a page for, and anything whose limits I would have had to guess.

## Live, hybrid or AI: what is the real trade-off?

A live human service wins on judgement, an AI service wins on booking and availability, and a hybrid tries to give you both. Here is how the three models differ on the things a practice feels.

Cost: live services bill by the minute or the call, so a busy month raises the bill. AI plans are usually a flat fee or an allowance. Booking: humans mostly take messages unless you pay for diary access; AI that is connected to your diary books during the call. After-hours: humans cost more outside office hours unless 24/7 is standard; AI answers at any hour by default. Upset callers: humans are better. My read is that an AI should recognise the call and hand it over, not improvise comfort.

Here is a worked example, labelled as an example and not client data. A practice has three phones ringing at 8.10am while the team is setting up, and a new patient ringing at 7pm. A message service takes four messages, and someone rings four people back the next day. An AI connected to the diary books the four, and the team arrives to a full day. Where I would still want a person is a patient who calls in tears about a bill.

## What do missed calls cost a dental practice?

Each unanswered call from a new patient is a booking that may go to the practice down the road, so the cost is the value of a new patient multiplied by the calls you miss. ## How do I choose?

Choose by what your callers want and when they ring, then test the shortlist with a real booking.

- Choose Automis if you want calls turned into diary entries and WhatsApp follow-up, with no project on your side.
- Choose Moneypenny if your patients want a trained person, and you can live with set hours or pay for extended cover.
- Choose Answer4u if overnight and weekend cover matters most and you are happy to get a quote.
- Choose TTNC if your call volume is low and you want a published price and no contract.
- Choose The VoIP Shop if you run Dentally or Aerona and want to try AI cover cheaply.
- Choose Softomate if you want a custom build with published pricing and you will scope the integrations yourself.

Whatever you pick, run three tests. Ask for a live booking in your own diary. Say "I have a swollen face" and watch where the call goes. Ask for the data processing agreement, because as the data controller the practice stays responsible under UK GDPR.
